# Curated remdesivir demonstration

These are real source excerpts with developer-prepared annotations, not synthetic
clinical observations and not an autonomous literature search. The profile only
supports NCT04280705. Each short excerpt was checked against its original retrieved
Europe PMC abstract. Original record hashes, retrieval times, access levels and
article-specific licence metadata are retained. Only short excerpts are packaged;
the original complete records remain in ignored runtime caches.

The three papers give overall clinical cardiac context, a healthy-participant QT
study and preclinical sex-dependent findings in guinea pigs. They do not establish
an increased human risk in women or resolve missing sex-stratified trial data.
Review source applicability and possible trial overlap. Prepared annotations are
explicitly labelled in the API/UI report; no internet searches are fabricated.
